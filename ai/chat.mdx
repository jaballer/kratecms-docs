KrateCMS includes a built-in AI chat powered by OpenAI, accessible directly from your admin panel. You can use it to brainstorm ideas, draft and refine content, or switch into agent mode and let the AI take real actions on your posts and pages — creating, searching, fetching, and updating content on your behalf.

## Opening AI chat

Navigate to **AI Chat** in the admin sidebar. The chat interface loads immediately — no additional configuration is required on your end.

<Note>
  AI chat is enabled per site. If you don't see **AI Chat** in your sidebar, it hasn't been turned on for your site yet — contact your platform administrator to request access.
</Note>

## Writing and sending messages

The composer works the way other AI chats do:

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Send your message | Press **Enter**, or click the send button |
| Start a new line | Press **Shift + Enter** |
| Copy a reply | Click the copy icon on the message; it confirms with a checkmark |
| Retry a reply | Click regenerate on the message to ask again |

The box starts one line tall and grows as you type, up to a point, then scrolls. Your message appears in the thread as soon as you send it, so you can see what you asked while the reply is being written. When the reply arrives, focus returns to the composer so you can keep typing.

<Note>
  On a phone or tablet, **Enter** inserts a new line instead of sending — use the send button. An on-screen keyboard has no practical Shift + Enter, so Enter has to stay available for writing.
</Note>

If a request fails — an expired session, a dropped connection, or a problem reaching the AI provider — the chat tells you what went wrong rather than silently dropping your message.

## Chat modes

KrateCMS AI chat offers six modes. Pick a mode from the selector at the top of the chat panel — each one tunes the assistant for a different task. You can switch between modes within the same session.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chat">
    The default conversational interface. Use it to ask questions, get writing feedback, brainstorm topics, or generate draft text you can copy into your posts.

    **Example prompts:**

    ```text
    Write me a 150-word introduction for a blog post about remote team culture.
    ```

    ```text
    Give me five title ideas for a post announcing our new pricing plans.
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email Copy">
    Generates email marketing copy — subject lines, body copy, calls to action — that you can paste straight into your email tool.

    ```text
    Write 5 subject lines for a flash sale on summer apparel.
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Images">
    Generates marketing images from a text prompt. Images appear in the chat panel; for the in-post featured-image generator, see [AI image generation](/ai/image-generation).

    ```text
    Hero image for a summer campaign — bright, cheerful, beach setting.
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Code">
    Generates short HTML and code snippets for marketing use — buttons, banners, embeds, small components.

    ```text
    HTML button that says "Shop Now" with rounded corners and a hover state.
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API Q&A">
    Lets you ask questions about JSON data from approved external URLs. Reference an endpoint inline with the `@` syntax — for example, `What's in @https://api.example.com/v1/products?` — and the assistant fetches the URL and answers from the JSON. Allowed hosts are configured by the platform operator under **Settings → Chat → API Read Allowed Hosts**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Agent">
    Agent mode gives the AI the ability to take actions inside your KrateCMS site. Instead of just generating text, the AI can work with your posts **and your pages** directly through tool-calling. There's no separate mode for each — describe what you want and the assistant picks the right surface.

    **Actions available on posts:**

    | Action | Permission needed |
    |---|---|
    | List posts | Create posts |
    | Search posts | Create posts |
    | Get a post by id or slug | Create posts |
    | Create a post (saved as a draft) | Create posts |
    | Update a post | Edit posts |

    **Actions available on pages:**

    | Action | Permission needed |
    |---|---|
    | List pages | View pages |
    | Search pages | View pages |
    | Get a page | View pages |
    | Create a page (saved as a draft) | Create pages |
    | Update a page | Edit pages |

    **Example prompts:**

    ```text
    List my last 5 published posts.
    ```

    ```text
    Search for posts about product launches.
    ```

    ```text
    Create a new draft post titled "Year in Review" with an introduction about our team's highlights.
    ```

    ```text
    Draft a new "Shipping & Returns" page covering our 30-day policy.
    ```

    <Note>
      You only see the tools your role allows. Contributors and authors work with posts but not pages; editors and above get both. A viewer has no agent tools at all, so agent mode has nothing to offer them.
    </Note>

    <Warning>
      Agent mode actions are real — they create and update actual content in your CMS. New posts and pages are always saved as **drafts**, so nothing the agent writes goes live until you review and publish it yourself. Authors and contributors can only act on their own posts, exactly as in the editor.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Conversation history and token usage

Your conversation history is maintained for the duration of your session. You can scroll back through the full exchange to review earlier messages or copy generated content.

Token usage for every message is recorded behind the scenes. Inline counts are not shown in the chat panel itself — to review per-message token usage and model costs, super-admins can open **Admin → Conversations** and inspect any conversation.

## Tenant scope

The AI agent operates entirely within your tenant's content. It can only see and act on the posts and pages that belong to your site — it cannot access other tenants' data or take actions outside your account. The agent enforces exactly the same permission and ownership rules as the editor: anything you couldn't do by hand, you can't do by asking.

## Tips for better results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be specific with your prompts">
    The more context you give, the more useful the response. Instead of "write a blog post," try "write a 300-word blog post introduction about the benefits of async communication for remote engineering teams, written for a non-technical audience."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use chat mode to draft, then copy into the editor">
    AI chat is a great place to generate raw content. Once you have something you like, copy it into the post editor and refine it before publishing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="In agent mode, confirm before publishing">
    When the AI creates or updates a post, it saves as a draft by default. Open the post editor to review, adjust, and set a publish date before making it live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Iterate on responses">
    If the first response isn't quite right, follow up with a refinement. For example: "Make that shorter" or "Change the tone to be more formal" — the AI retains the conversation context.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

  <Card title="Bulk updates" icon="pencil">
